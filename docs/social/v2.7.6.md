# ColaMD v2.7.6 Social Copy

发布方式：正式版。

## Chinese

ColaMD 2.7.6 发布了。这一版只改布局，修一个把阅读位置吃掉的回归。

**打开文件（大纲）面板后，正文不再被面板压住。** 面板是浮在窗口上的一列，从 2.7.3 起正文忘了给它让位：面板一开，正文就被盖住一截，面板越宽盖得越多，滚动条也跟着藏在面板底下。现在正文按面板所在的那一侧让开，并且在剩下的可见区域里居中，面板放左边、放右边都对。切到源码模式（`⌘/`）时文字不会横向跳，两种模式落在同一个阅读位置上。

感谢 @a39908646 的报告（#143）。

如果你也遇到面板挡住正文，升级到 2.7.6 就好。

## English

ColaMD 2.7.6 is out. This release only touches layout, and fixes a regression that ate into the reading position.

**With the file (or outline) panel open, the text is no longer covered by it.** The panel is a column that floats over the window, and since 2.7.3 the text had stopped making room for it: opening the panel covered the end of every line, wider panels covered more, and the scrollbar ended up hidden behind the panel too. The text now steps aside on whichever side the panel sits and stays centred in what is left, with the panel docked left or right. Switching to source mode (`Cmd+/`) no longer shifts the text sideways either: both views sit on the same reading position.

Thanks to @a39908646 for the report (#143).

If the panel has been covering your text too, 2.7.6 is the fix.
